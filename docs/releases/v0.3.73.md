# OJ NEXUS v0.3.73 — Training Calibration

## Included

- Stores optional default and per-judge training difficulty targets in DataStore.
- Adds localized Focus Sprint calibration controls with validation and clear actions.
- Builds a bounded, deterministic candidate pool from due reviews, recent unsolved problems,
  failure history, and knowledge-linked problems.
- Adds bounded low-mastery evidence, judge-specific difficulty fit, and explicit explanation codes
  to training recommendations.

## Safety boundary

The change remains local-first. It adds no inferred rating target, automatic submission,
credentials, cookies, passwords, new network response, or problem-content persistence.
